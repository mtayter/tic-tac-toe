Privacy Policy — Tic-Tac-Toe

Effective: October 1, 2026

Tic-Tac-Toe ("the app") is a two-player pass-and-play game for Wear OS watches. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Information we collect

None. The app does not create accounts, does not use analytics, does not serve ads, and does not include any third-party SDKs. The app requests no permissions at all — not even internet access — so it has no technical means of transmitting data off your device.

Game state

The state of your game (the marks on the board, whose turn it is) exists only in the app's memory while you play. It is never saved, never recorded, and never transmitted.

Information sharing

We share no information with anyone, because we have no information to share.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
